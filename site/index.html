What it does

Bluetooth that understands what you mean.

Regular Bluetooth makes you speak its language: device names like WH-1000XM5 and file names you have to remember. Redtooth works from what you actually say.

connect to Sam’s speaker

Say whose it is.

No asking what it’s called or guessing between two identical names. If Sam uses Redtooth and marked the speaker as theirs, Redtooth knows which one you mean.

✓ Sound is on Sam’s speaker
send Daniel my resume

Say what you mean.

Not the file name. Redtooth searches what’s inside your files, what you’ve opened and sent, and when you edited them, so CV_2026_final (2).pdf is found as “my resume”.

✓ Saved to Daniel’s laptop
the deck I edited this morning

Asks only when it matters.

If two files fit, Redtooth asks the one question that tells them apart, like “the Q4 board update or the sales kickoff?”, and remembers your answer next time.

✓ Q4 Board Update.pptx

How it works

Set up once. Then just ask.

  1. Install

    Redtooth sits in your system tray and opens in your browser. Pair headphones and speakers right from it.

  2. Add your number

    Friends who use Redtooth find your devices through your phone number, the same way they already know you.

  3. Mark your devices

    Tell Redtooth which headphones, speakers and phone are yours, and the name friends use for them. Then ask for anything.

Files go straight from your device to theirs over your Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth, never through a server, and the other person always taps Accept first.

Download

Get Redtooth.

Windows Beta

One installer, about two minutes to set up. Installs for your user only, no admin rights needed.

Download Redtooth-Setup.exe

Windows will say it protected your PC because the installer isn’t signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Then open Redtooth from the Start menu, enter your name and number, and press Enter to use the shared directory. All releases

macOS and Linux

From the command line, with Python 3.11 or newer.

pip install git+https://github.com/AdetayoKalejaiye/redtooth
redtooth setup && redtooth start

macOS also needs brew install blueutil switchaudio-osx.

Questions

Good to know.

Does the other person need Redtooth?

To connect to their devices by name or send them files, yes: they mark their devices as theirs and accept what you send. Your own Android phone can receive files over Bluetooth without Redtooth. iPhones can’t receive Bluetooth files at all, so use AirDrop for those.

Do my files go through a server?

No. Files go device to device over your Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth, so you need to be near each other or on the same network. Search runs on your computer too; nothing about your files is uploaded.

What does Redtooth store about me?

A shared directory keeps your name, your phone number, and the devices you’ve chosen to publish, so friends can find them. It doesn’t store your contacts, your files, or what you send to whom. When Redtooth checks which of your contacts use it, it sends hashes of their numbers, not the numbers. The privacy section has the details.

Is my phone number verified?

Not yet. The first person to register a number keeps it, and nobody can register it after that, so set Redtooth up early. Your other computers join with a one-time link code.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet, which costs money and paperwork that a beta doesn’t have. Click More info, then Run anyway. The code is all on GitHub.
